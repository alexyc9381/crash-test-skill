# Bulletproof Plan: Keep It Alive Plant Club

Date: 2026-10-20. This is a thinking aid made by language models, not a forecast, and not financial, legal or tax advice. Numbers marked (estimate) were worked out from guesses; numbers marked (assumed) are guesses. This plan includes the fixes from the re-test.

## The plan
- **What it is:**
  - A "Keep It Alive" plant club.
  - The first box holds one hard-to-kill houseplant and a care card.
  - Care texts are written ahead of time and scheduled for each plant.
  - New plants ship on one clear schedule, written in the terms. If a box is held back for weather, the customer gets a credit.
  - A written guarantee replaces any plant that dies in its first 90 days.
- **Who pays:** US apartment renters who want greenery but keep killing plants. They pay for help and a guarantee, not for a pile of plants. The first customers come from inside the 2-day UPS zone around the nurseries.
- **Price and cost to deliver:**
  - **Main plan:** $147 prepaid per quarter. Each plant costs ~$24-36 to deliver (assumed). Margin is ~$63-87 per quarter (estimate) if 2 plants ship, before ads and the founder's time.
    - The terms must say exactly how many plants a quarter buys. "Every other month" works out to 1.5 plants, so pick one rule and say it.
  - **Pause or downgrade plan:** ~$69 per quarter (estimate) for one plant plus the texts. This gives people a way out other than cancelling.
- **How customers find it:**
  - First, groups and DMs.
  - Then a capped Meta ad test shown only inside the 2-day zone.
  - Then influencer codes.
  - Customers who tick a separate consent box join the founder's own SMS list. Care texts and promo texts stay separate, and STOP works automatically.
- **What it depends on:**
  - Two Florida nurseries, plus one backup nursery outside Florida.
  - Drop-shipping for every plant. Home packing is only a fallback.
  - UPS Ground inside the 2-day zone, shipping Monday and Tuesday only. Boxes are held when the forecast is under ~40°F or over ~90°F (assumed), and heat packs go in during winter.
  - A written answer on state plant-shipping rules.
  - Pet-safe plants, or clear labels on any that aren't.
  - Clear auto-renew terms with one-click cancel.
  - A paid backup person who answers guarantee claims within 48 hours.
- **Money and time:** Solo founder, $15,000, 20 hours a week. Budget (estimate):
  - ~$500 for compliance
  - ~$250 for the transit test
  - $500 for the first ad test
  - Up to $5,000 on ads before the kill-switch check
  - The rest held as reserve and for first inventory

**Kill switches** (stop spending if any of these happen):
- Fewer than 10 strangers pay in the 7-day test.
- Each customer costs more than ~$63 (estimate) after $1,500 of ads.
- Fewer than 50% renew after the first quarter (assumed bar).
- More than 10% of boxes arrive dead in any month (assumed bar).
- $7,500 is spent without reaching 50 paying subscribers (assumed bar).

## Fix plan
1. **NO CUSTOMERS / PRODUCT FIGHTS ITS BUYER**
   - **Change:** Sell keeping plants alive, not more plants.
   - **Test:** A real, refundable $147 checkout. Run ads only in the 2-day zone, post in groups, and DM 50 strangers. Run it once with a rival's $25 box shown next to the offer.
   - **Pass:** 10 or more strangers pay within 7 days, at $63 or less of ad spend per buyer.
   - **Time:** 1-2 weeks.
2. **PRICING / CHURN**
   - **Change:** Prepaid quarters, plus a pause-or-downgrade plan, and a capped ad test.
   - **Test:** Run the pause option in 20 DMs.
   - **Pass:** 60% or more of people who would cancel choose pause or downgrade instead.
   - **Time:** 1 week.
3. **QUALITY / DEAD ON ARRIVAL**
   - **Change:** A 2-day shipping zone, Monday-Tuesday shipping, weather holds, a written guarantee, and one written shipping rule.
   - **Test:** Ship 6 boxes to the hot and cold edges of the zone.
   - **Pass:** 6 of 6 healthy on day 7 and $36 or less each to deliver, and 18 of 20 people explain the terms correctly.
   - **Time:** 2-3 weeks.
4. **NO MOAT**
   - **Change:** Make the moat the care texts and a customer list the founder owns.
   - **Test:** Offer the first 30 subscribers $39 without texts or $49 with them.
   - **Pass:** 60% or more are on the texted plan after the first quarter.
   - **Time:** 1 quarter.
5. **DEPENDENCY: SUPPLY AND THE FOUNDER**
   - **Change:** Add a backup nursery outside Florida, drop-ship every plant, pre-schedule care texts, and pay a backup person for claims.
   - **Test:** Call 3 nurseries, then run a 30-day hands-off test.
   - **Pass:** One nursery outside Florida can ship for $12 or less within 7 days. During the hands-off month, every text goes out, no ship dates are missed, and every claim is answered within 48 hours.
   - **Time:** 1 week, then 30 days.
6. **LEGAL**
   - **Change:** Get a plant-shipping compliance answer, use pet-safe plants, add a separate SMS consent box with automatic STOP, and write clear auto-renew terms.
   - **Test:** Email the Florida Department of Agriculture's plant industry office, then audit the first 20 checkouts.
   - **Pass:** Compliance costs under ~$500 (estimate). All 20 checkouts have a timestamped consent record, and no texts go out after a STOP. Ask a lawyer to check the guarantee, auto-renew and SMS terms.
   - **Time:** 2-4 weeks, running alongside the other fixes.
